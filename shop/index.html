The pause

There it is. That pause.

That half-second between silence and sensation, when the world exhales and something deeper enters. Still & Social was born from that moment. This candle is a way to bring it home.

A ritual in three parts

Come sit. Come slow down.

01

Light

Strike a match at the end of the day. Let the first flame mark the line between doing and being.

02

Pour

Brew a cup, put the phone face down and stay with the warmth in your hands.

03

Pause

No performance, just presence. Come as you are, and leave a little more whole.
