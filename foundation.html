Our Why

Hospitality has always meant more to us.

To us, hospitality has never been only about what is placed on the table. It is about how someone feels. Welcomed. Seen. Connected. Cared for.

That belief sits at the heart of Still & Social. Creating meaningful moments that bring people together and leave them feeling a little more whole.

The Still & Social Foundation is a natural extension of that spirit, carrying care beyond our spaces and into the community around us.

Through nourishment, shared experiences, thoughtful gestures and community partnerships, we hope to create more moments of connection, kindness and belonging.

What We Do

Small acts. Meaningful impact.

Nourish

Creating moments of nourishment that bring warmth, comfort and care beyond our table.

Connect

Bringing people and community together through meaningful relationships and shared experiences.

Care

Thoughtful gestures, given with intention, to create moments where people feel seen, valued and considered.

Grow

Listening to our community and thoughtfully evolving the ways we contribute to wellbeing, connection and belonging.

Current Initiative

Meals With Meaning

On selected evenings, the heart of Still & Social reaches beyond our table and into the community around us.

We intentionally dedicate time to crafting fresh, nourishing meals for our local community.

Through trusted local organisations, these meals are shared within our community, allowing the warmth of Still & Social to reach a little further.

A meal prepared with care. A moment shared with meaning.

Where
Monbulk and the Dandenong Ranges
When
Selected evenings each month
Alongside
Local community organisations
Beyond Our Shores

Further From Home

Care, carried a little further.

Our sense of community extends beyond where we live.

Thailand holds a meaningful place in the story of Still & Social. Through our Foundation, we support local schools and communities in Southern Thailand through education, essential supplies and nourishing meals.

It is a simple extension of what we believe in. Creating moments of care, nourishment and opportunity, wherever they may be.

With the support of our Still & Social community, we are grateful to carry this care a little further.

From our community to another, with care.

Our Philosophy

We believe care has a ripple effect.

One thoughtful gesture. One meaningful connection. One moment of care.

Small moments of care can stay with us, creating a sense of connection that carries far beyond the moment itself.

Looking Ahead

This is only the beginning.

Our hope is simple. To keep finding meaningful ways to care, connect and give back, both close to home and further beyond it.

As the Still & Social Foundation grows, we hope to reach more people, create more moments that matter and continue finding thoughtful ways to make a difference.

We will grow gently, with heart, purpose and gratitude for everyone who helps make it possible.

Small beginnings, with meaningful intentions.

Community & Partnerships

Better, together.

We believe meaningful care begins with connection.

We would love to hear from local organisations, businesses and individuals who share our heart for community and would like to create something meaningful together.
